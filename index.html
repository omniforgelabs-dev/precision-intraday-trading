Work through the modules in order. Each one builds on the last, and several carry practical exercises whose output is used in the next module.

Before you beginWhat this course is, and is not

What it is

A structural framework for intraday decision-making. It teaches you where to look, what constitutes evidence, where risk is defined, and — most importantly — when to do nothing.

What it is not

A signal service, a set of guaranteed setups, or a promise of profitability. No method removes uncertainty. This one aims to make your uncertainty measurable and your risk defined.

The language throughout is deliberately probabilistic. You will not read “this always works.” You will read “higher-probability,” “valid setup,” “favourable conditions.” That is not hedging — certainty language causes oversizing and refusal to exit, and the vocabulary shapes the behaviour.

Educational material only. Nothing here is financial advice, a solicitation, or a promise of results. Trading involves substantial risk of loss. All charts are illustrative and synthetic; they depict decision logic, not historical performance. Test any method in simulation before risking capital.
